# Surface brief: main game screen (mock)

Scope: one Operate-mode screen, the live game HUD. Static mock at frontend/mock_frontend.html; four reviewable states (Ready, Flying, Ejected, Aborted).
Audience: hackathon judges on a projector/laptop; casual players in short sessions. Job: bet, watch, decide when to eject, read the outcome instantly.
Constraints: brief-pinned tone (mission control meets modern game, deep space, one accent); no agency marks; EN/FR; reduced motion; WCAG AA both themes.
Grounded list (resonance order): 1 ascent-profile trajectory plots, 2 long-exposure launch streaks, 3 1970s graphics-standards manuals, 4 star atlases, 5 press-site countdown clocks, 6 mission patches, 7 first-day space covers. Assigned: 4, star atlas, translated into the pinned world.
Challenger verdicts: centre-rail reference (declined, kept one-pixel hairline rule system); jet-age ticket wallet (competitive, kept "nothing disappears, it cancels": history entries keep their outcome); character catalog (declined, kept fixed fields per entry); Crouwel grid (declined, kept the grid as a real armature: the graticule is the plot's true axes); Saville sleeve (declined, kept vast unprinted ground with one plot owning the field); Ikeda datamatics (declined, kept tabular numerals at data density).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The flight is plotted like a night-sky chart: altitude is charted on a true graticule, whole multipliers are catalogued like stars, the abort is a marked nova. Refuses the neon line-chart-in-a-casino default.
OWN-WORLD: Deep-space navy ground, pale chart ink, hairline graticule at one weight, one flare-amber accent for the live trajectory and the single primary action; mint (eject) and coral (abort) only as state colour with icons. Day mode is the printed atlas: pale chart paper, navy ink. Archivo across widths; tabular numerals.
STORY: Player sees balance and phase instantly, sets wager, launches, watches the plotted climb, ejects, and reads a one-line mission-control outcome.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (mark, name, balance, EN/FR, theme). Left two-thirds: chart stage with rocket, trail, giant multiplier top-left, status line beneath. Right column: wager, chips, auto eject, Place bet/Eject. Mission log strip under the stage.
FORM: star atlas, position 4 of 7, seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
